## Kat Roemmich, PhD

I’m an interdisciplinary information scientist specializing in privacy, human-AI interaction, and AI governance. I bridge mixed methods with normative theory to identify and measure risks in applied AI and design socio-technical interventions to mitigate them, with a particular focus on the future of work and healthcare. Grounded in a decade of work in enterprise information systems where I implemented emerging technologies and governance programs for healthcare and workforce management, my research develops pathways to align AI policy and practice with human values in contexts of rapid innovation and operational complexity.  I completed my PhD in Information at the University of Michigan. You can reach me at <roemmich@umich.edu>.
