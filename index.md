## Kat Roemmich, PhD

I’m an interdisciplinary information scientist specializing in human-AI interaction, with a particular focus on privacy and AI governance in the future of work and healthcare. I combine mixed methods to identify, measure, and mitigate socio-technical risks, and normative theory to evaluate them for human agency, dignity, and privacy considerations. Grounded in a decade of work in enterprise information systems where I implemented emerging technologies and governance programs for healthcare and workforce management, my research develops pathways to align AI policy and practice with human values in contexts of rapid innovation and operational complexity.  I completed my PhD in Information at the University of Michigan. You can reach me at <roemmich@umich.edu>.
